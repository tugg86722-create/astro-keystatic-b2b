## Technical Engineering Guide: Hydrogen & High-Pressure Fluid Handling

When operating at pressures exceeding 5,000 PSI, seal extrusion, explosive decompression, and hydrogen embrittlement become the primary failure modes.

### 1. Material Compatibility (Hydrogen Embrittlement)
Austenitic stainless steels with nickel contents above 12% (such as 316L) exhibit substantially lower susceptibility to hydrogen embrittlement compared to standard 304 stainless steel.

### 2. Bubble-Tight Seat Integrity
Standard elastomeric O-rings can suffer rapid gas decompression (RGD). We recommend reinforced PEEK or virgin PCTFE seats rated for cryogenic through elevated temperature cycles.
